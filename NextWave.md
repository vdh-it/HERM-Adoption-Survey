# Next Wave: Reaching the Countries Not Yet Covered

**Created:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** proposal for the EA SIG; nothing has been sent yet. `Distribution.md` remains the working document for links and tracking. Invitation and reminder texts stay in `Survey_Invitation.md` (Parts 1–3). No new texts are proposed here.
**Inputs:** `Countries.md` (47 entries), `Distribution.md` (tracking table, 2026-11-03), `National_Higher_Education_IT_Networks_Europe.md` (inventory of 7 and 8 October 2026), an aggregate count from the LamaPoll export of 2026-11-03, and web checks of 2026-11-03 for the micro-states and Bosnia and Herzegovina (sources in section 8).

## 1. The gap

`Countries.md` lists 47 countries. The tracking table in `Distribution.md` covers 24 of them (AL, AT, BE, CH, CZ, DE, DK, EE, ES, FI, FR, GB, GR, HR, IE, IT, LV, NL, NO, PL, PT, SE, SI, SK). There is **no channel for 23 countries**:

AD, BA, BG, BY, CY, HU, IS, LI, LT, LU, MC, MD, ME, MK, MT, RO, RS, RU, SM, TR, UA, VA, XK

Currently, institutions in these countries can only be reached through the EUNIS channel. According to `Distribution.md` section 7, the EUNIS member map lists one institution each in RO and LU and none in the other 21 countries.

**Note on NRENs as distributors:** NRENs operate networks. Their regular contacts at member institutions are usually network and IT operations staff, not CIOs or enterprise architects. They are a good **entry point**, but the forwarding has to be addressed explicitly to IT leadership. Ideally, ask the NREN for a contact to the national IT leadership forum or the rectors' conference.

## 3. Feasibility

| Group | Countries | Analysis value (Analysis Matrix E1: country comparison only with ≥ 5 institutions) | Feasibility | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: large systems** | TR, RO, HU, UA, BG, RS, LT | ≥ 5 institutions per country realistic if the distributor forwards the invitation | medium: an NREN exists everywhere, but in most cases there is no IT leadership association in the inventory, and no contact person is known yet | **Priority.** These countries close the largest gap in coverage (Central and South-Eastern Europe, Türkiye, Ukraine). |
| **B: medium and small systems** | BA, MK, MD, XK, CY, ME, MT, IS, LU | per country mostly < 5, so they count as "Other countries" in the analysis | good where the NREN is closely tied to the main university (ME, MT, IS, LU, XK, CY); difficult in BA (no NREN) | **Second priority.** Adds coverage, not country analyses. | **C: micro-states** | AD, LI, MC, SM, VA | 0–2 institutions each, analytically negligible | very good: direct contact to one to three institutions | **Low effort, symbolic value.** One mail to the university management or IT department is enough. In MC the only university is a private business school. Whether it fits the target group is a judgement call. VA: respondents from pontifical universities may choose IT in question 1.2, which is accepted (question 1.2 is authoritative). |
| **D: no active outreach (proposal)** | RU, BY | — | politically not appropriate | **Proposal: do not invite actively.** Reason: the suspended European research cooperation. Responses through open channels remain possible. **Decision for the EA SIG or the EUNIS Board.** |

**Realistic expected outcome:** in group A, for a few countries ≥ 5 institutions (most likely RO, TR, HU, if the partners forward). Groups B and C together add perhaps 10–20 institutions, which belong to "Other countries". Taken as a whole, the next wave mainly widens geographical coverage. It will not make up for the low response in the large countries that already have a channel (section 4).

## 4. The larger lever: countries with a channel but without responses

This means that **FR, IT, ES, GB and NL**, the largest higher education systems in the country list, have **no response yet** although channels exist (AMUE, CSIESR, CINECA, Crue, SIGMA, Jisc, UCISA, SURF). The same applies to AT, BE, DK, EE, FI, GR, HR, IE, LV, PT, SI, SK and AL.

Consequence for the plan: in the same weeks as the new wave, apply step 6 of `Distribution.md` (reminder with priority for channels below 5 institutions) consistently to these channels. For partners marked "endorsed" or "invited", first clarify whether they have forwarded the invitation at all.

## 6. Plan

**Texts for NRENs:** Part 4 (request to the NREN) and Part 5 (request for institutional input to official addresses) in `Survey_Invitation.md`; notes on use in Part 6.

### Additional routes that reach several countries at once

1. **EUNIS Board / EUNIS secretariat:** ask for introductions to NRENs or rectors' conferences in group A. Contacts that come through the Board have worked better than cold mails (cf. the endorsements in `Distribution.md`).
2. **GÉANT community:** all NRENs of groups A and B (except BA) belong to the GÉANT community. A single request to GÉANT to forward the invitation to its NREN members could replace several cold contacts. This has not been checked; it is an option.
3. **Partners already reached in neighbouring countries:** ask ARNES (SI), SRCE (HR) and RASH (AL) for contacts in the Western Balkans; ask CESNET / EUNIS-CZ and EUNIS-SK for contacts in HU.
4. **EUNIS member institutions in RO and LU** (one each, `Distribution.md` §7): ask them to forward the invitation nationally.
5. **European Universities alliances** and the **EU DigInterOp group** (`Ideas.md`): secondary routes for groups A and B. Their selection bias is unknown, so sending institution-wide to all members is not recommended.

### Closing date: decision for the EA SIG (step 7)

A wave sent in week 46 needs about three weeks of response time plus the reminder. The closing date must be announced at least two weeks in advance.

- **Option 1, close in mid-December 2026 (for example, Friday 2026-12-18):** final reminder by 2026-12-04 at the latest. The new wave gets about 4–5 weeks, which is tight but possible. Advantage: the field phase is not much longer than planned internally. Risk: the pre-Christmas period lowers responses.
- **Option 2, close in mid-January 2027:** takes the Christmas break into account and gives the new wave and the reminders to the large countries enough time. Disadvantage: the field phase is extended by about one month, and analysis starts later.

The internal two-month plan stays internal; partners only receive the closing date (`Distribution.md` §2).
