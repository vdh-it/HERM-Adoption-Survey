**Subject:** Can your NREN help? European survey on Enterprise Architecture in universities

Dear colleagues,

As President of EUNIS, I would like to ask for your help with a European survey being conducted by our Enterprise Architecture Special Interest Group. This is the first survey of its kind in Europe, asking universities how they plan and organise their processes, information and IT, what holds them back and what kind of support would be helpful. While more than 100 institutions from 15 countries have participated thus far, many countries are still underrepresented, especially in Southern, Central and Eastern Europe, the Baltic states and the Western Balkans.

**What is this about?**
Enterprise Architecture (EA) illustrates the relationship between a university's objectives, activities, data, and IT systems. Universities use EA to inform investment decisions, determine which systems to replace or share, and avoid duplication of efforts.
Higher Education Reference Models (HERM), published by CAUDIT (the Australasian Association of University IT Directors), provide a shared vocabulary for how universities apply EA.
Together, EA and HERM can help universities plan their digital transformation and assist NRENs in developing services that meet university needs and can be shared across institutions.
You don't need to know anything about EA to contribute. Universities with no formal EA experience are just as welcome to participate, and their responses are important for achieving an accurate picture.

**How your NREN can help:**
- Pass the invitation on to the CIO or head of IT at the institutions you work with. If your usual contacts are involved in networking or identity and access management, please ask them to forward the invitation to the people responsible for IT strategy.
- Alternatively, simply tell us who in your country is better placed for distribution, such as a national forum of IT directors or the rectors' conference. An introduction would be much appreciated.

If you would like to help, please contact Markus von der Heyde (markus.von.der.heyde@semalogic.de), who is leading the survey for the EA SIG. He will send you your own survey link and a ready-made invitation that you can translate and adapt. You don't need to share any contact data. Once at least five institutions have participated, your country will be shown separately in the results, which illustrate where universities identify gaps in EA that impact areas such as integration, shared services, and IT governance.

Many thanks!

Evelien Renders
President, EUNIS
