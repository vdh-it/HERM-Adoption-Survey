# HERM Usage Survey: Understanding EA Adoption in Higher Education

## Project Vision
**Goal:** Understand and improve Enterprise Architecture (EA) adoption in Higher Education institutions by studying real-world HERM usage patterns, motivations, and outcomes.

**Core Question:** What are the many "Whys" driving EA adoption in HE? Who uses HERM as a core artifact collection, for what purposes, and with what outcomes?

## Project Phases

### Phase 1: Case Study Collection & Analysis (Current)
- Systematic collection of HERM adoption case studies from HE institutions
- Analysis of motivations, implementation patterns, and outcomes
- Identification of success factors and common challenges
- Development of a case study taxonomy

### Phase 2: Research Outputs
- Comprehensive research whitepaper/report
- Academic publication(s) on EA adoption drivers in HE

### Phase 3: Knowledge Base & Resources
- Interactive case study database & knowledge base
- Best practices & guidance materials
- Educational frameworks for EA adoption

## Target Audience
1. **Primary:** HERM practitioners & active adopters in HE
2. **Secondary:** EA/IT leadership in HE institutions
