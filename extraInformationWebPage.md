## FAQ

### What is the survey about, and who runs it?

The survey explores how higher education institutions in Europe adopt and use Enterprise Architecture (EA) and the [Higher Education Reference Model (HERM)](https://www.caudit.edu.au/communities/caudit-higher-education-reference-models/). It covers current practices, use cases, barriers and support needs.

The study is conducted in close connection with the [EUNIS Enterprise Architecture Special Interest Group (EA SIG)](https://eunis.org/task-forces/enterprise-architecture/), which promotes knowledge sharing and the joint development of EA practice across higher education. The findings will be discussed with the EA SIG first and then published to support further community activities.

### What kind of questions can I expect?

A few questions cover you and your institution: your role, your organisational unit, how well you know EA at your institution, and the country, type and size of your institution. Most questions are about Enterprise Architecture practice at your institution, if there is one. We also ask about your experience with HERM: how it is used, what has worked and why, and what is missing. Finally, we ask about barriers and about the support that would help institutions build their EA capability, so that the community can focus its efforts where they help most.

### How long does it take?

Most participants need between 10 and 20 minutes; so far, the typical time has been about 12 minutes. The survey shows only the questions that apply to your institution: if EA or HERM play a small role there, you will finish faster. If you share detailed HERM experience, it takes a little longer.

### Can I pause the survey and continue later?

Yes. At the bottom of each page, there is a small link to save your current state. It gives you a personal link with which you can continue later where you left off. Please keep this link private, because anyone who has it can open your response.

The link has a second use: if you later want to withdraw your consent but did not give your institution's name or an e-mail address, the link lets us find your response (see [Withdrawing your consent](#Withdrawing-your-consent)).

### Can I see the complete questionnaire before I take part?

Yes. On the first page, choose "Preview the questionnaire only". Your preview answers are not included in the study.

Please note that the survey adapts to your answers, for example to your role, to the status of EA at your institution and to your experience with HERM. Each participant therefore sees only part of the questionnaire, and no single path shows all questions. If you only click "Next" in the preview, you will skip most of the survey, including all questions on how HERM is used. To see the in-depth questions, choose "Established operational practice" as the status of Enterprise Architecture and "Active operational use" as the level of HERM adoption.

Our suggestion: start the survey directly. It shows only what is relevant to you, and a preview followed by the real survey usually takes longer than answering once. If you stop part-way, the answers you have given so far still count, and you can also [pause and continue later](#Can-I-pause-the-survey-and-continue-later).

### My institution has no Enterprise Architecture practice. Should I still take part?

Yes, please. Your answers are just as important as those from institutions with an established EA practice. We want a realistic picture of European higher education, not only of the frontrunners. For institutions without EA practice, the survey is noticeably shorter.

### Can several colleagues from my institution take part?

Yes. Colleagues in different roles often see EA at their institution differently, and all responses are kept. In our analysis, we can count each institution only once where this matters (see step 6 in [How is the data cleaned before analysis and publication?](#How-is-the-data-cleaned-before-analysis-and-publication)).

### What if I don't know the answer to a question?

You answer on behalf of your institution to the best of your knowledge. Where it is reasonable not to know, you can choose "Don't know / Cannot assess". Many questions are optional.

### Why is the survey only in English?

We invite institutions from all over Europe and want everyone to answer exactly the same questions. A single language ensures that the answers can be compared.

### How long does the survey stay open?

The survey will stay open for several weeks, because national networks across Europe are still distributing it. Once the distribution is complete, we will send a final reminder that announces the closing date at least two weeks in advance.

### Why do you ask for the name of my institution?

The name is optional. It helps us recognise several responses from the same institution and spelling variants. It is published only if you agree at the end of the survey (see step 4 in [How is the data cleaned before analysis and publication?](#How-is-the-data-cleaned-before-analysis-and-publication)).

### Can my institution be identified in the published data?

- **If you give the name of your institution and agree at the end of the survey that it may be named:** yes. The institution is named in the published dataset.
- **Otherwise:** not directly. The name is removed, and no contact details or technical data are published. However, a combination of characteristics, such as country, type and size of the institution and the distribution channel, may in rare cases allow someone to guess which institution responded, especially in countries with few higher education institutions.

To reduce this risk, we apply the same rule in our reports and in the published dataset: a country is shown separately only if at least five institutions from that country have taken part. All other countries are combined into one group, "Other countries". The distribution channel is treated in the same way: it is shown only for channels through which at least five institutions responded.

### Can my national network see my answers?

Your national network or community can see the responses received through its own survey link in the published, de-identified dataset, like anyone else, provided that at least five institutions responded through this link. It never receives your contact details or your institution's name unless you allowed the name to be published. Results for a distribution channel are likewise reported to the partner only if at least five institutions responded through it.

### How is the data cleaned before analysis and publication?

Before we analyse the data, and before we share it with anyone, including the EA SIG, we prepare it in the following steps.

1. **Test runs, previews and declined participation are removed.** Test responses from before the start of the survey, and responses from people who chose "Preview the questionnaire only" or "Leave the survey" on the first page, are not part of the study. Comments on the survey itself, given on the last page, are used only to improve the survey and are not published.
2. **Personal and technical data are removed.** We delete your contact e-mail address and the technical information recorded by the survey platform: the exact date and time of your response, your device, operating system and browser, and the web page you came from. We keep only the calendar week in which you responded, so that we can follow how responses come in during the survey period. The time you needed to complete the survey is used only internally to check data quality and is not published.
3. **Contact details are kept separately.** If you give an e-mail address for follow-up activities or peer matching, it is stored in a separate list with restricted access. This list never becomes part of the research dataset and is never published.
4. **Your institution is named only with your permission.** The name of your institution is optional. If you give it, it remains in the published data only if you answer "Yes" to the question on naming your institution at the end of the survey. Otherwise, the name is removed.
5. **Comments are de-identified.** Before free-text answers are analysed, quoted or published, we remove names, places and other details that could identify you or your institution.
6. **Answers are checked, not changed.** We check the answers for consistency. Implausible combinations are marked for our analysis, but your answers are not altered. If several colleagues from one institution take part, all responses are kept; we do not merge them, but we mark them so that each institution can be counted only once where this matters.
7. **Incomplete responses count.** If you stop before the end, the answers you have given up to that point are included in the analysis.
8. **Country and distribution channel are shown only for groups of at least five institutions.** National networks and communities each received their own survey link. The published data shows through which channel a response arrived, so that partners can analyse the responses from their own community. This marker identifies the channel, never the individual person. A country or a channel with fewer than five participating institutions is combined with others into one group (see [Can my institution be identified in the published data?](#Can-my-institution-be-identified-in-the-published-data)).
