Dear Evelien, dear EUNIS Board,

As discussed, we are preparing the distribution of the EUNIS EA/HERM survey and would like to take up the Board's kind offer of support.

**We would like to ask the Board for an endorsement message:**

Could you Evelien, on behalf of the Board and CC to us, send a short introductory email to the national/member organisations (e.g. ZKI, UCISA, EUNIS-CZ, SURF), endorsing the survey and asking them to actively support its distribution?

**Why we propose this approach**

We would like to distribute the survey mainly through the national organisations, rather than only to institutions that are direct EUNIS members. These organisations can reach their communities directly and explain why taking part matters for their own members.

Each participating organisation would receive a dedicated survey link. This lets us identify the distribution channel and, where there are enough responses, give the organisation specific feedback on its own community alongside the overall European results. The survey then becomes useful evidence for them: how EA and HERM are currently used among their members, which use cases and barriers exist, and where future guidance, exchange formats or community activities would add the most value.

**What we would provide**

For each organisation, we would supply:

1. its dedicated survey link
2. an English master invitation text, which they can translate or adapt and send to their members in their usual national language(s); the questionnaire itself stays in English
3. the survey dates and a short set of practical instructions

Where possible, we would also ask each organisation for the approximate number of institutions or members reached, so that participation per channel can be interpreted properly.

Many thanks again for the Board's support.
