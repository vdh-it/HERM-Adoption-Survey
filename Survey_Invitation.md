## Part 1: Letter to the national organisation

**Subject:** EUNIS survey on Enterprise Architecture and HERM: your community's results depend on your call

Dear [CONTACT NAME],

Following the endorsement message from the EUNIS Board, I am writing to invite [ORGANISATION] to become a distribution partner for the European survey on the use of Enterprise Architecture (EA) and the Higher Education Reference Model (HERM) in higher education. The survey is run by the EUNIS Enterprise Architecture Special Interest Group (EA SIG).

### Why your organisation matters

We deliberately do not rely only on EUNIS mailing lists. National organisations know their communities, reach them directly and can explain why participation matters from their own perspective. Your call to your members is therefore the single most important factor for a high response rate.

### What [ORGANISATION] gains

The survey is designed so that each distribution partner can use the results for its own community, not only for the European picture:

- **A dataset you can analyse yourself.** [ORGANISATION] has its own survey link. Responses received through it carry a channel marker that identifies the distribution channel, never the individual respondent. The de-identified dataset will be published openly on Zenodo under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and keeps this marker for every channel through which at least five institutions responded. You can therefore filter your community's responses and analyse them with your own questions, for example on EA status, HERM adoption, barriers or support needs of your members.
- **A benchmark against Europe.** You can compare your community directly with the overall European results and with other countries.
- **Feedback from us.** Where enough responses come through your channel, we will provide a short summary of your community's results alongside the European report. We invite you to discuss the results with us and the EA SIG, and to contribute to the interpretation for your country.
- **Evidence for your own planning.** The results show where guidance, examples, exchange formats or community activities around EA would add the most value for your members.

The value of all of this depends on how many of your members respond. A single channel or country is shown separately in the results and in the published dataset only if **at least five institutions** from it take part; smaller groups are pooled, for example into "Other countries". Your call therefore decides whether your community becomes visible in the results.

### What we ask you to do

1. **Send the invitation soon** to your relevant member channels, using `[MAIN LINK]`. The survey is already open, so the sooner your members receive it, the better. Please adapt the template invitation below and, ideally, translate it into the language you normally use with your members. The questionnaire itself is in English.
2. **Add a few sentences on why** the results matter for your own community. This makes a measurable difference to participation.
3. **Send a reminder later.** We will contact you when a reminder would help in your community and send you a short text together with a separate reminder link. Please let us know now whether you can send such a reminder to your members.
4. **Tell us roughly how many** institutions or relevant members received the invitation, so that participation through your channel can be interpreted properly.
5. **Use only the provided link.** This is what allows us to attribute responses to your community.

### Timing

The survey is open now. To keep momentum, we ask you to follow this timing:

- **Your members are asked to respond within three weeks** of receiving your invitation. The template invitation below already says so.
- **We announce the closing date at least two weeks in advance.** A final reminder will then be sent through your channel and through the EUNIS mailing lists.

### Who should take part

We invite people with insight into EA or IT strategy at their institution, for example enterprise, business or solution architects, CIOs and heads of IT, digital strategy or digital office staff, and IT programme managers. Institutions without any EA practice are explicitly welcome; their answers are just as important. Several people from the same institution may respond, since different units often have different views.

The questionnaire adapts to each respondent. Depending on their answers, it skips questions that do not apply and goes into more depth where an institution has experience with EA or HERM. Most respondents need 10 to 20 minutes (typically about 12): noticeably less for institutions with little EA or HERM experience, and more for those who share detailed HERM use cases.

### Data protection

Participation is voluntary and based on consent under the GDPR. The data controller is SemaLogic, Weimar, Germany. Contact details and other direct identifiers are never published. Institutions are named only if respondents explicitly agree. Full information, including a short FAQ, is provided on a separate page linked from the first page of the survey.

Many thanks for supporting this work. I am happy to answer any questions or to discuss the survey in a short call.

Best regards,

Markus von der Heyde
Principal Investigator, on behalf of the EUNIS Enterprise Architecture SIG
SemaLogic · markus.von.der.heyde@semalogic.de

## Part 6: Notes on use of Parts 4 and 5 (internal)

1. **No obligation can be created by wording.** EUNIS is an association, not a public authority. No English formula makes a reply legally mandatory. Do not invoke Freedom of Information legislation: it gives access to information an institution already holds, not a duty to answer a questionnaire. Bindingness comes from routing, authority and a clear request: the official address, a signatory with standing, an action box, a date and an easy way to decline.
2. **"Consultation" is used in the sense of a sector consultation by EUNIS.** The letter states that it is carried out as a research survey, so that it is consistent with the survey's introduction text and the consent in question 0.1. The text must not suggest a mandate by a ministry or the EU. The EA SIG should confirm the term before use.
3. **Declining by e-mail, not via the survey.** The survey's "Leave" option (0.1) leads directly to Section 9 without asking for the institution, and such responses are excluded (Blueprint data rule 13). A decline through the survey therefore cannot be attributed. Ask for a reply e-mail instead. British administrative usage calls this a "nil return"; for non-native readers the plain paraphrase in Part 5 is clearer.
4. **[RESPONSE DATE] is a public date.** It must be three weeks after sending at the earliest and must not be later than the closing date decided by the EA SIG (`NextWave.md` §6). This partly departs from the internal rule above (no fixed end date that is later extended). Set it only once the closing date is decided, and do not move it afterwards.
5. **[REFERENCE]** e.g. `EUNIS-EA-2026-[ISO]-[nn]`, one per institution if sent directly, otherwise one per channel. Record it in `EUNIS_contacts.csv`, not in Git.
6. **Measuring the effect of the framing:** if Part 5 is sent directly to official addresses, use a separate channel name (e.g. `Distribution_Official_<ISO>`; check with the LamaPoll attribute setup first). This lets us compare the response to the consultation framing with the partner channels. Apply rule 4 of `Distribution.md` before sending.
7. **Signatory:** a signature or co-signature on behalf of the EUNIS Board, or by the NREN itself, substantially increases the chance that rectors' offices route the request. Agree this with the Board before the first send.

3. **If a forward is not possible**, we would be just as grateful for the name of the right person in GÉANT's education or community area, or for a short slot or post in the Education SIG.
