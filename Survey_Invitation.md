## Part 1: Letter to the national organisation

**Subject:** EUNIS survey on Enterprise Architecture and HERM: your community's results depend on your call

Dear [CONTACT NAME],

Following the endorsement message from the EUNIS Board, I am writing to invite [ORGANISATION] to become a distribution partner for the European survey on the use of Enterprise Architecture (EA) and the Higher Education Reference Model (HERM) in higher education. The survey is run by the EUNIS Enterprise Architecture Special Interest Group (EA SIG).

### Why your organisation matters

We deliberately do not rely only on EUNIS mailing lists. National organisations know their communities, reach them directly and can explain why participation matters from their own perspective. Your call to your members is therefore the single most important factor for a high response rate.

### What [ORGANISATION] gains

The survey is designed so that each distribution partner can use the results for its own community, not only for the European picture:

- **A dataset you can analyse yourself.** [ORGANISATION] has its own survey link. Responses received through it carry a channel marker that identifies the distribution channel, never the individual respondent. The de-identified dataset will be published openly on Zenodo under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and keeps this marker. You can therefore filter your community's responses and analyse them with your own questions, for example on EA status, HERM adoption, barriers or support needs of your members.
- **A benchmark against Europe.** You can compare your community directly with the overall European results and with other countries.
- **Feedback from us.** Where enough responses come through your channel, we will provide a short summary of your community's results alongside the European report. We invite you to discuss the results with us and the EA SIG, and to contribute to the interpretation for your country.
- **Evidence for your own planning.** The results show where guidance, examples, exchange formats or community activities around EA would add the most value for your members.

The value of all of this depends on how many of your members respond. Results for a single channel or country can only be reported separately if **at least five institutions** from it take part; smaller groups are pooled into "Other countries". Your call therefore decides whether your community becomes visible in the results.

### What we ask you to do

1. **Send the invitation by [SEND-BY DATE]** to your relevant member channels, using `[MAIN LINK]`. The survey is already open, so the sooner your members receive it, the better. Please adapt the master text in Part 2 and, ideally, translate it into the language you normally use with your members. The questionnaire itself is in English.
2. **Add a few sentences on why** the results matter for your own community. This makes a measurable difference to participation.
3. **Send a reminder later.** We will contact you when a reminder would help in your community and send you a short text together with a separate reminder link. Please let us know now whether you can send such a reminder to your members.
4. **Tell us roughly how many** institutions or relevant members received the invitation, so that participation through your channel can be interpreted properly.
5. **Use only your own links.** They are what allows us to attribute responses to your community.

### Timing

The survey is open now. To keep momentum, we ask you to follow this timing:

- **Please send your invitation by [SEND-BY DATE].** Experience shows that most responses arrive within a few days of an invitation, so an early mailing makes the biggest difference.
- **Your members are asked to respond within three weeks** of receiving your invitation. The master text in Part 2 already says so.
- **We follow up with reminders.** We monitor participation per channel and will contact you with a reminder text and link when a reminder would help in your community.
- **We announce the closing date at least two weeks in advance.** A final reminder will then be sent through your channel and through the EUNIS mailing lists.

### Who should take part

We invite people with insight into EA or IT strategy at their institution, for example enterprise, business or solution architects, CIOs and heads of IT, digital strategy or digital office staff, and IT programme managers. Institutions without any EA practice are explicitly welcome; their answers are just as important. Several people from the same institution may respond, since different units often have different views.

The questionnaire adapts to each respondent. Depending on their answers, it skips questions that do not apply and goes into more depth where an institution has experience with EA or HERM. It takes about 20 minutes on average: noticeably less for institutions with little EA or HERM experience, and more for those who share detailed HERM use cases. Please mention this in your invitation, so that colleagues without EA experience are not put off.

### Data protection

Participation is voluntary and based on consent under the GDPR. The data controller is SemaLogic, Weimar, Germany. Contact details and other direct identifiers are never published. Institutions are named only if respondents explicitly agree. Full information is provided on the first page of the survey.

Many thanks for supporting this work. I am happy to answer any questions or to discuss the survey in a short call.

Best regards,

Markus von der Heyde
Principal Investigator, on behalf of the EUNIS Enterprise Architecture SIG
SemaLogic · markus.von.der.heyde@semalogic.de
